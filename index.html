Shortcut tricks

×5, ×25, ×125

×5 = ×10 ÷ 2: 46×5 = 460÷2 = 230
×25 = ×100 ÷ 4: 36×25 = 900
×125 = ×1000 ÷ 8: 24×125 = 3000

×9, ×99, ×11

47×9 = 470−47 = 423 · 35×99 = 3500−35 = 3465
×11: add the digits in the middle: 36×11 = 3(3+6)6 = 396; carry if needed: 78×11 = 858

Squares

Ending in 5: n×(n+1) then 25 → 35² = 1225
Near 50: 2500+100d+d² → 53² = 2809
Near 100: 10000−200d+d² → 97² = 9409
(a±b)²: 47² = 2500−300+9 = 2209

Two numbers near 100

Use deficits: 96×93 → 4, 7 → (96−7)(4×7) = 8928

Same tens, units add to 10

47×43 → 4×5 | 7×3 → 2021

Percentage

x% of y = y% of x. 15% = 10% + 5%. Successive changes a and b: a + b + ab/100. +20% then −20% = −4%.

Divide by 5, 25, 125

÷5 = ×2 ÷10: 345÷5 = 69 · ÷25 = ×4 ÷100 · ÷125 = ×8 ÷1000

Divisibility

3/9: digit sum · 4: last two digits · 8: last three · 11: difference of alternate digit sums · 7: double the last digit and subtract.

Exam strategy

Estimate when options are far apart, back-solve from the options, skip anything over 60 seconds, and write in aligned columns.

Alphabet positions & opposites

Anchor letters EJOTY: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25.
Position from the right = 27 − position from the left: K is 11th from A → 27−11 = 16th from Z
Opposite letters add up to 27 (A↔Z, B↔Y … M↔N): opposite of F → 27−6 = 21 = U

Letter series

Turn letters into numbers, find the gap, turn back. Also test alternating gaps (+2, +3, +2 …) and two interleaved series.
B D G K → 2, 4, 7, 11 → gaps 2, 3, 4 → next gap 5 → 16 = P

Number series

Check in this order: constant difference, constant ratio, growing differences, squares/cubes ± a number, two interleaved series.
3, 4, 6, 9, 13 → gaps 1, 2, 3, 4 → next gap 5 → 18

Digit sums

48275 → 4+8+2+7+5 = 26
A number and its digit sum leave the same remainder when divided by 9, so it is a quick way to check an answer.
